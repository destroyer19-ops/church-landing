What is Foundation School?

Christ Embassy Foundation School is a structured spiritual training program designed to teach the foundational doctrines of Christ. It helps believers understand who they are in Christ, grow in spiritual maturity, and become effective witnesses of the Gospel.

Foundation School is open to:

  • New converts seeking spiritual growth
  • Church members desiring deeper spiritual understanding
  • Anyone desiring a solid foundation in God’s Word

Key Focus Areas

  • The New Creation

    Understanding our identity and new life in Christ.

  • The Holy Spirit

    Discovering the person and work of the Holy Spirit in us.

  • Christian Doctrine & Evangelism

    Foundational teachings and effective soul-winning strategies.

  • The Local Church & Ministry

    Our role and purpose within the body of Christ and ministry.
