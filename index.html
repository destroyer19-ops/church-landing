Who We Are

Christ Embassy Barking is a place where lives are transformed through the teaching of God’s Word, the power of the Holy Spirit, and heartfelt worship. We are committed to raising spiritually grounded believers equipped to live victorious and purposeful lives in Christ.

Teaching the Word

We are passionate about teaching the Word with clarity and power.

Raising Leaders

We are passionate about raising leaders and ministers.

What Is Foundation School?

A structured spiritual training program designed to teach the foundational doctrines of Christ, helping believers grow in spiritual maturity and become effective witnesses.

  • The Holy Spirit
  • Christian Doctrine & Evangelism
